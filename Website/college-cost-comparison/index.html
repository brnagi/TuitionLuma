College cost comparison

Compare college costs beyond tuition.

TuitionLuma helps students and families compare the real cost of college, including tuition, fees, housing, financial aid, debt, and outcomes.

What to compare

A school with lower tuition is not always the less expensive choice. Compare the total bill, the aid package, how much students borrow, and whether graduates finish with strong outcomes.

  • Tuition and required fees
  • Housing, meals, books, and transportation
  • Grants, scholarships, work-study, and family contribution
  • Student debt and monthly loan payment estimates
  • Graduation rates, earnings, and major outcomes

FAQ

What is the best way to compare college costs?

Start with total cost of attendance, subtract grants and scholarships, then compare likely borrowing and outcomes.

Should I compare debt between schools?

Yes. Debt affects monthly payments after graduation and can change how affordable a school feels long term.

Can two schools with similar tuition have different real costs?

Yes. Housing, aid, fees, and completion rates can make similar tuition numbers lead to very different family costs.
