What we focus on

Less guessing. More clarity.

College planning can be dense, so TuitionLuma emphasizes clear cost labels, readable summaries, and controls that communicate what is selected.

  • Clear VoiceOver labels for important actions like Save, Compare, Edit Profile, Change Program, Restore Purchase, and Apply Estimate.
  • Readable cost, aid, debt, earnings, and outcome summaries that do not rely only on color.
  • Visible selected states for profile preferences, planning mode, scenario modeling, program choices, and other toggle-like controls.
  • Text that can wrap where possible, especially for school names, recommendation explanations, and college-planning guidance.
  • Standard iOS navigation patterns, including tab navigation and native sheets.

Known limitations

TuitionLuma is still improving. Some data-dense views, charts, exported reports, or third-party college data may need continued accessibility refinement. We do not currently claim custom support for every accessibility setting, such as Reduce Motion, color filters, captions, or audio descriptions.
