Cost overview

Texas A&M University-College Station reports an average net price of $21K. Tuition is $13K for in-state students and $40K for out-of-state students where reported. Families should compare this against grants, scholarships, housing, and likely borrowing.

Outcomes overview

Reported median earnings are $72K. This is not a guaranteed salary, but it helps compare the long-term payoff side of the college decision.

Debt discussion

Median debt for completers is $18K. A lower debt load can make monthly repayment easier after graduation, especially when earnings are still uncertain.

Graduation discussion

The reported graduation rate is 84%. Completion matters because extra time in school can increase tuition, living costs, and borrowing.
