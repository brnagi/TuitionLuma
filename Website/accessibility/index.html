Accessibility

Accessibility at TuitionLuma

TuitionLuma is built to help students and families make college decisions with clearer, more readable information. We aim to make the app usable with standard iOS accessibility features and continue improving accessibility as the product evolves.

Supported app accessibility features

TuitionLuma uses native iOS and SwiftUI patterns for navigation, forms, buttons, lists, and controls. The app includes VoiceOver-friendly labels, values, hints, grouped summaries, and selected-state indicators across key areas such as Explore, School Detail, Compare, Calculator, Aid, Saved Schools, Profile, and Pro purchase screens.

The app also supports Dynamic Type through system text styles in most views, readable contrast improvements, keyboard-friendly forms, tappable controls with visible states, and screen reader summaries for school metrics, LumaScore, cost estimates, calculator results, and program cards.

What we focus on

  • Clear VoiceOver labels for important actions like Save, Compare, Edit Profile, Change Program, Restore Purchase, and Apply Estimate.
  • Readable cost, aid, debt, earnings, and outcome summaries that do not rely only on color.
  • Visible selected states for profile preferences, planning mode, scenario modeling, program choices, and other toggle-like controls.
  • Text that can wrap where possible, especially for school names, recommendation explanations, and college-planning guidance.
  • Standard iOS navigation patterns, including tab navigation and native sheets.

Known limitations

TuitionLuma is still improving. Some data-dense views, charts, exported reports, or third-party college data may need continued accessibility refinement. We do not currently claim custom support for every accessibility setting, such as Reduce Motion, color filters, captions, or audio descriptions.

Feedback and support

If you experience an accessibility issue, please contact us and include your device, iOS version, assistive technology used, and the screen where the issue occurred.
