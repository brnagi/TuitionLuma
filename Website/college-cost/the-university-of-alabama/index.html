Cost overview

The University of Alabama reports an average net price of $22K. Tuition is $12K for in-state students and $34K for out-of-state students where reported. Families should compare this against grants, scholarships, housing, and likely borrowing.

Outcomes overview

Reported median earnings are $59K. This is not a guaranteed salary, but it helps compare the long-term payoff side of the college decision.

Debt discussion

Median debt for completers is $23K. A lower debt load can make monthly repayment easier after graduation, especially when earnings are still uncertain.

Graduation discussion

The reported graduation rate is 73%. Completion matters because extra time in school can increase tuition, living costs, and borrowing.

Who should consider this school?

The University of Alabama may be worth a closer look for students who want a public option and plan to compare personalized aid, debt, and program outcomes before committing. It may require extra review if your estimated net cost is far above the reported average or if your intended program has limited outcome data.
