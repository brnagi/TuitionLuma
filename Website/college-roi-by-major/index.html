What shapes ROI

A college decision is not just about the school name. Major, credential, completion path, earnings, and debt can all affect long-term value.

  • Program-level earnings where reported
  • Program debt and repayment pressure
  • School-wide graduation and affordability data
  • Major-aware recommendations for intended fields
  • Plain-language ROI signals for family discussion

FAQ

Can the same school have different ROI by major?

Yes. Program earnings and debt can vary across fields of study.

Should ROI be the only decision factor?

No. ROI is useful, but students should also consider fit, support, completion likelihood, and career goals.

What happens when program data is missing?

Use school-wide outcomes as a backup and confirm details with the college or program department.
