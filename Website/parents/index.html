FAQ

What numbers matter most for parents?

Net price, expected borrowing, monthly payment estimates, graduation rates, and likely outcomes are all important.

Can a family report help with college conversations?

Yes. A clear report makes it easier to compare options without relying on scattered tabs and spreadsheets.

Should parents compare major outcomes?

Yes, especially when a student has a likely field of study. Program earnings and debt can change the value picture.
