Editorial comparison

University of Colorado Boulder and University of Minnesota-Twin Cities ask families to weigh different parts of the college decision. On affordability, University of Minnesota-Twin Cities has the lower reported average net price at $17K. For long-term earnings, University of Colorado Boulder has the higher reported median earnings at $70K. Debt also deserves a separate look: University of Colorado Boulder reports the lower median completer debt at $20K. Graduation data points toward University of Minnesota-Twin Cities with a reported rate of 85%, which may matter for students trying to limit extra semesters and additional borrowing.

The overall tradeoff is not simply which school has the lowest cost or the highest earnings number. A student with a strong aid offer may reasonably choose the school that fits their academic path, while a family focused on repayment comfort may prioritize net price and debt. Based on the available data, TuitionLuma leans toward University of Minnesota-Twin Cities because of lower reported average net price, and higher reported graduation rate. Students should compare this result with actual financial aid letters, housing plans, and intended programs before deciding.
