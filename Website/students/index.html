FAQ

Should students compare cost before applying?

Yes. Cost planning can help create a balanced list of affordable, stretch, and high-risk options.

Can intended major affect recommendations?

Yes. Major outcomes can make school recommendations more personal when program data is available.

Should students still care about campus fit?

Absolutely. TuitionLuma adds financial clarity, but students should also consider support, location, culture, and goals.
