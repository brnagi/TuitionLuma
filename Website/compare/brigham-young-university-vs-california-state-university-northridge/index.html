Editorial comparison

Brigham Young University and California State University-Northridge ask families to weigh different parts of the college decision. On affordability, California State University-Northridge has the lower reported average net price at $7K. For long-term earnings, Brigham Young University has the higher reported median earnings at $76K. Debt also deserves a separate look: Brigham Young University reports the lower median completer debt at $11K. Graduation data points toward Brigham Young University with a reported rate of 81%, which may matter for students trying to limit extra semesters and additional borrowing.

The overall tradeoff is not simply which school has the lowest cost or the highest earnings number. A student with a strong aid offer may reasonably choose the school that fits their academic path, while a family focused on repayment comfort may prioritize net price and debt. Based on the available data, TuitionLuma leans toward Brigham Young University because of higher reported median earnings, lower reported median debt, and higher reported graduation rate. Students should compare this result with actual financial aid letters, housing plans, and intended programs before deciding.
