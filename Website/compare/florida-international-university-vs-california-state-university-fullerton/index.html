Editorial comparison

Florida International University and California State University-Fullerton ask families to weigh different parts of the college decision. On affordability, California State University-Fullerton has the lower reported average net price at $7K. For long-term earnings, California State University-Fullerton has the higher reported median earnings at $63K. Debt also deserves a separate look: California State University-Fullerton reports the lower median completer debt at $14K. Graduation data points toward Florida International University with a reported rate of 74%, which may matter for students trying to limit extra semesters and additional borrowing.

The overall tradeoff is not simply which school has the lowest cost or the highest earnings number. A student with a strong aid offer may reasonably choose the school that fits their academic path, while a family focused on repayment comfort may prioritize net price and debt. Based on the available data, TuitionLuma leans toward California State University-Fullerton because of lower reported average net price, higher reported median earnings, and lower reported median debt. Students should compare this result with actual financial aid letters, housing plans, and intended programs before deciding.
