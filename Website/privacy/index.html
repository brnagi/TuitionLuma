Privacy Policy

TuitionLuma is designed as a College Decision Assistant that helps families understand college choices while keeping personal planning details limited and practical.

Information stored on your device

TuitionLuma may store saved schools, compared schools, student profile details, calculator settings, preferred programs, and saved repayment plans locally on your device. Student profile fields may include nickname, GPA, optional SAT or ACT information, state residency, intended major, family income range, debt tolerance, public/private preference, distance from home preference, campus size preference, and learning format preference.

Saved repayment plans are stored locally on your device so you can revisit loan term, monthly payment, total repayment, and scenario details in the app.

College data sources

TuitionLuma uses College Scorecard data from the U.S. Department of Education where available. School searches and college detail requests may send school names, state filters, or selected school identifiers to the College Scorecard API.

Third-party images and links

TuitionLuma may load public school, state, logo, or website image assets from external providers or school websites. These requests can reveal basic technical information such as IP address, device type, and requested image URL to those providers.

Purchases

TuitionLuma Pro is planned as a one-time purchase. In-app purchases are handled by Apple, and purchase management is subject to Apple's payment and refund policies.

No sale of personal information

Incisive Labs LLC does not sell personal information. TuitionLuma does not use advertising trackers.

Contact

Questions about TuitionLuma privacy can be sent to support@tuitionluma.com.
